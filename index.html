01 / OUR VISION

Mission Overview

IRON MAN is our proposed satellite telemetry demonstration. Its purpose is to collect sensor measurements, transmit data to a ground station, and present the information through a web dashboard. CHAMPAK is the name of our assistant system.

Our main goal: Build a working miniature satellite system that demonstrates how onboard electronics, sensors, communication, and ground monitoring can work together.

03 / HARDWARE PLAN

Components & Their Roles

ESP32 DevKit

Reads sensors and performs processing for the satellite-side prototype.

Arduino Uno R3

Can be used for ground-station control or a separate demonstration.

ESP32-CAM

Captures images and may provide camera streaming over Wi-Fi.

MPU6050

Measures acceleration and rotation to demonstrate movement and orientation.

BH1750

Measures light intensity for a light-monitoring demonstration.

BMP280

Measures atmospheric pressure and temperature. It cannot measure vacuum.

LoRa SX1278 RA-02

A long-range radio option for transmitting small telemetry packets.

NRF24L01+ PA+LNA

An alternative radio option for data transmission. It is not a sensor.

04 / TELEMETRY LINK

Communication System

The satellite prototype will send small data packets to a receiving ground station. The laptop will display the received measurements.

Option A: LoRa

SX1278 RA-02 modules can provide a long-range, low-data-rate radio link when correctly configured.

LONG-RANGE OPTION

Option B: NRF24L01+

The PA+LNA version is another radio option. Its actual range depends on antennas, power, interference, and the environment.

ALTERNATIVE OPTION
Team decision: Choose one radio system for the first working link. Both radio types require compatible modules, suitable antennas, correct wiring, and matching firmware at both ends.

Important Technical Details

  • RA-02 SX1278 and NRF24L01+ modules use 3.3 V power and logic.
  • Use an appropriate stable power supply for the selected radio.
  • Do not connect 5 V Arduino signals directly to 3.3 V-only radio inputs.
  • Camera images require substantially more bandwidth than small sensor readings.
  • Radio operation must follow applicable local frequency and power rules.

06 / TEAM STRUCTURE

Four Members, One Mission

🔧

Person 1: Components

Manage sensors, microcontrollers, wiring, power requirements, component testing, and hardware assembly.

💻

Person 2: Webpage

Develop the GitHub website, dashboard interface, telemetry display, and future live-data integration.

🎨

Person 3: Design

Build the satellite body, ground-station model, component layout, labels, and exhibition presentation.

📡

Person 4: Communication

Configure the chosen radio, test data transfer, verify telemetry, and connect the ground station to the laptop.

All four members should participate in final integration and testing. The communication and webpage members will coordinate to display real telemetry once the hardware link is operational.
